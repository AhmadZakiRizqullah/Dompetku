# Anti-Slop Audit 001 (2026-11-21)

Project: Money Tracker (Aplikasi Pencatatan Keuangan)
Design Direction: UI/UX Style Guide (Modern & Minimalist, Warm & Trustworthy #FDFBEE / #015551 / #57B4BA / #FE4F2D)
Audit Target: Mobile Layout, UI Components, Typography, Interaction Affordances, and State Resilience

---

## Numbered Findings List

### Finding 01
- **File:** `lib/presentation/settings/settings_page.dart:591`
- **Rule:** R-02 (Copywriting: Em Dash Character Forbidden)
- **Priority:** HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Reason:** Contains the forbidden em dash character (`—`) in date range separator text instead of a hyphen (`-`) or localized separator (`s/d`).

### Finding 02
- **File:** `lib/presentation/dashboard/dashboard_page.dart:262`
- **Rule:** R-26 (Interactive Elements: Non-Functional Controls)
- **Priority:** HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Reason:** Recent transaction items have an empty `onTap: () {}` callback that performs no action, presenting dead interactive elements to the user.

### Finding 03
- **File:** `lib/presentation/settings/settings_page.dart:198`
- **Rule:** R-26 (Interactive Elements: Non-Functional Controls)
- **Priority:** HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Reason:** The app info tile displays a forward chevron arrow indicating navigation but has a dead `onTap: () {}` callback.

### Finding 04
- **File:** `lib/presentation/category/category_page.dart` & `lib/presentation/settings/settings_page.dart`
- **Rule:** R-24 (Navigation: Orphan Screen Without Accessible Route)
- **Priority:** HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Reason:** `CategoryPage` is fully implemented for income and expense categories but has zero navigation links or entry points anywhere in the app.

### Finding 05
- **File:** `lib/presentation/dashboard/dashboard_page.dart:338-372`, `transaction_history_page.dart:55-57`, `wallet_list_page.dart:73-77`
- **Rule:** R-27 (UI States: Missing Cause and Next Action in Empty/Error/Loading States)
- **Priority:** HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Reason:** Loading states use raw uninformative boxes without shimmering/context, and error states display raw error strings with no recovery/retry action.

### Finding 06
- **File:** `lib/presentation/wallet/wallet_list_page.dart:231-252, 276-287`
- **Rule:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness: Under-Sized Touch Targets and Cramped Actions)
- **Priority:** HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Reason:** Wallet item action buttons have a tap target of only 28x28 px with 4px spacing, violating the 44x44 px minimum mobile touch target guideline.

### Finding 07
- **File:** `lib/presentation/statistics/statistics_page.dart:125-154, 173-203`
- **Rule:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness: Multi-Column Row Without Mobile Reflow)
- **Priority:** HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Reason:** Three summary cards are forced side-by-side in a rigid horizontal Row, resulting in cramped ~57px card content and text collision on narrow screens.

### Finding 08
- **File:** `lib/presentation/wallet/wallet_list_page.dart:59-72, 81-85`
- **Rule:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness: Fixed Bars and Floating Action Buttons Obscuring Content)
- **Priority:** HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Reason:** `WalletListPage` lacks bottom list padding to account for the Floating Action Button and bottom navigation bar, causing list items at the bottom to be cut off.

### Finding 09
- **File:** `lib/presentation/dashboard/dashboard_page.dart:44` & `lib/presentation/settings/settings_page.dart:449`
- **Rule:** R-04 (Icons & Decoration: Decorative Emojis in UI Copy)
- **Priority:** MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)
- **Reason:** Uses literal decorative emojis (`👋`, `🔔`) in system greetings and feedback copy, conflicting with clean typographical craft.

### Finding 10
- **File:** `lib/presentation/widgets/total_balance_card.dart:115` & `lib/core/theme/app_theme.dart:23-34`
- **Rule:** R-01 / R-29 (Color Palette: Unthemed Neon Accents and Oversaturated Chart Palette)
- **Priority:** MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)
- **Reason:** Total balance card hardcodes neon green `0xFF06D6A0` against deep teal, and chart colors use 10 miscellaneous colors outside the warm brand system.

### Finding 11
- **File:** `lib/presentation/dashboard/dashboard_page.dart:279-283`
- **Rule:** R-11 / R-20 (Border Radius: Floating Action Button Shape Inconsistency)
- **Priority:** LOW (Quality Locks)
- **Reason:** Uses `FloatingActionButton.extended` resulting in an elongated capsule pill rather than the defined rounded square (`borderRadius: 18`) in the style guide.

### Finding 12
- **File:** `lib/presentation/dashboard/dashboard_page.dart:86-88, 115-116, 192-193`
- **Rule:** R-05 (Layout & Spacing: Compounded Margins During Loading State)
- **Priority:** LOW (Quality Locks)
- **Reason:** Nested padding and container margin compound to 40px during loading states, causing content to visibly shift horizontally when data finishes loading.

### Finding 13
- **File:** `lib/main.dart:89-95` & `lib/presentation/dashboard/dashboard_page.dart:156-163`
- **Rule:** R-05 / R-24 (Layout Structure: Redundant Nested Navigation Stack for Bottom Nav Destination)
- **Priority:** LOW (Quality Locks)
- **Reason:** "Lihat Semua" wallet pushes `WalletListPage` onto the navigation stack with duplicate AppBars while Wallet is already a direct bottom tab destination.
